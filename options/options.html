Rito Settings

Configure voice behavior, accessibility, and custom command shortcuts.

Groq connection (required)

Rito uses Groq to understand your voice. Get a free key at console.groq.com/keys and paste it below.

Speech

Lower = more forgiving (catches quieter speech). Higher = stricter (reduces false positives).

In wake phrase mode, Rito keeps listening but only executes commands after hearing a wake phrase such as "hey rito" or "hello rito".

AI page summaries (optional)

Uses your Groq key to power "summarize this page". Requests run through the background worker so your key stays out of web pages.
